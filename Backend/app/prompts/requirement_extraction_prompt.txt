STEP 2 — CONFIRMATION RULES
================================================================================

A requirement IS confirmed if {{CLIENT_NAME}}:
  - States it directly in any sentence
  - Responds to a team member's proposal with ANY of:
      "Yes", "Correct", "Right", "Okay", "Go ahead", "Do that", "Roger",
      "Let's go ahead", "That's right", "Exactly", "For sure", "Absolutely",
      "That has been the case", "That's what I mentioned", "Agreed",
      "Make that change", "Fix that", "We need to", "Put it in production"

A requirement is NOT confirmed if {{CLIENT_NAME}}:
  - Says "No", "Don't", "Not yet", "Let me think", "I'm not sure"
  - Does not respond at all to the topic

CRITICAL REMINDERS — READ BEFORE GENERATING OUTPUT
================================================================================

1. Negative requirements ("must not", "should not", "do not show") are SEPARATE items.

   "Client name looks read-only" → extract it.

   "When to send the email" ≠ "What the email must contain"

5. Testing scenarios are separate items.

6. Validation rules are separate items.

7. Process rules are separate items.

8. Do NOT invent requirements not discussed.
